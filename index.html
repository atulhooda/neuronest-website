In the clinic

The kit,
in plain words.

No mystery machines. Here is what we use and what it is for, so you know before you walk in.

  1. 01

    Audiometer

    Measures how well your child hears each pitch and volume. Headphones on, a beep, a raised hand. It takes minutes and does not hurt.

  2. 02

    Electrical stimulation

    Gentle stimulation of the throat muscles, used alongside exercises to help retrain swallowing after illness, surgery or neurological injury.

  3. 03

    Hearing aids

    For children and adults with hearing loss. Try one in the clinic first, then we fit and tune it until it feels right.

Before you visit

Quick answers.

What most families ask before their first visit. Anything else, call or WhatsApp.

Do you only see children?

Most of our work is with children, but we also see adults for swallowing therapy (including after cancer treatment), voice therapy and neuro-communication therapy after a stroke or brain injury.

My child isn't talking yet. Where do we start?

With an assessment. We usually check hearing first, because a hearing gap is a common and easily missed cause of speech delay. Then we look at language, play and attention.

Can you test hearing here?

Yes. We have an audiometer in the clinic for hearing evaluations, and we offer hearing aid trials and fitting.

Does my child need more than one therapy?

Sometimes. If they do, it happens under one roof with one plan, so speech, OT and physio goals support each other instead of competing.
