About this therapy

What it is.

Behavioural therapy helps children with tantrums, aggression, difficulty following routines, or challenges linked to autism and ADHD. It starts by understanding what a behaviour is communicating, then teaches new skills that meet the same need in a better way.

Parents are a big part of it. We share strategies you can use at home, so the same approach carries through the whole day.

Signs it might help

  • Frequent or intense tantrums
  • Hitting, biting or other aggressive behaviour
  • Difficulty with transitions or changes to routine
  • Trouble sitting, waiting or taking turns
  • Behaviour that is affecting school or family life

In a session

What it looks like.

Every plan is built around the person in front of us, but most sessions include these.

  1. 01

    Understanding why

    Observation and a conversation about when behaviours happen.

  2. 02

    Teaching new skills

    Structured play for waiting, sharing and asking for help.

  3. 03

    Catching the good

    Positive reinforcement of the behaviour you want to see.

  4. 04

    A plan for home

    Clear, simple steps you can use every day.

Families ask

Good questions.

Is this just a phase?

Some behaviour is part of growing up. If it is frequent, intense or getting in the way of daily life, it is worth talking to us.

Is behavioural therapy only for autism?

No. It helps many children, with or without a diagnosis.

Will I be involved?

Yes. Parent involvement is one of the biggest factors in progress.
