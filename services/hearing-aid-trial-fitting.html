About this therapy

What it is.

If a hearing test shows a loss that hearing aids can help, you can try one in the clinic before deciding. We look at your type of hearing loss, your daily life and your budget, and explain the options without jargon.

Once you choose, we fit and programme the hearing aid to your test results, then fine-tune it over follow-up visits until it sounds right.

Signs it might help

  • A hearing test has shown hearing loss
  • Struggling to follow conversations, even when people speak clearly
  • Family say you often miss things
  • An existing hearing aid that no longer feels right

Families ask

Good questions.

Can I try a hearing aid before buying?

Yes. The trial is there so you can hear the difference first.

Are hearing aids suitable for children?

Yes. Children with hearing loss often benefit a great deal, especially for speech and language development.

How long does it take to get used to one?

Usually a few weeks. Sounds can seem loud at first, and follow-up adjustments help.
