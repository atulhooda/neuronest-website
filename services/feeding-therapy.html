About this therapy

What it is.

Feeding therapy helps children who find eating difficult, whether that is because of how their mouth muscles work, sensitivity to textures, or worry around food. It goes beyond ordinary fussy eating: these children often accept very few foods, gag easily, or find every mealtime stressful.

We work step by step, starting from what your child can already manage and building towards new tastes and textures, without pressure.

Signs it might help

  • Eats a very small range of foods
  • Gags, vomits or refuses new textures
  • Difficulty chewing or moving food around the mouth
  • Very long or very stressful mealtimes
  • Stuck on purées and not moving to lumpy or solid food

Families ask

Good questions.

Is my child just a picky eater?

Many children go through fussy phases. If eating is affecting growth, health or family life, or the list of accepted foods keeps shrinking, it is worth an assessment.

Will you force my child to eat?

No. Pressure usually makes feeding harder. Children explore new foods at their own pace.

Should I be in the session?

Usually, yes. Most meals happen at home, so you are a big part of the plan.
