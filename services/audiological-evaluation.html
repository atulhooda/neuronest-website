About this therapy

What it is.

An audiological evaluation measures how well someone hears across different pitches and volumes. We test with an audiometer in the clinic, including pure tone audiometry: you listen through headphones and respond each time you hear a beep.

For children, hearing is often the first thing to check when speech is delayed. A mild hearing loss can be easy to miss at home but make a big difference to how a child learns words.

Signs it might help

  • Speech delay or unclear speech
  • Not responding when called, or often saying "what?"
  • Turning the TV up loud
  • Difficulty following conversation in noisy places
  • Ringing in the ears, or a sudden change in hearing

Families ask

Good questions.

Does a hearing test hurt?

No. You simply listen and respond. It is quick and comfortable.

Can young children be tested?

Yes. We adapt the test to your child's age and make it feel like a listening game.

What if a hearing loss is found?

We explain what it means, and where suitable you can try a hearing aid in the clinic.
