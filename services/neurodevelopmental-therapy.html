About this therapy

What it is.

Neurodevelopmental therapy (NDT) is a hands-on approach for children with conditions that affect movement and posture, such as cerebral palsy, developmental delay or brain injury. The therapist guides the child's movements to help them feel and learn more typical patterns.

The aim is practical: better sitting, standing, reaching and walking, so children can take part more fully in play, school and family life.

Signs it might help

  • A diagnosis of cerebral palsy or another neurological condition
  • Stiff, floppy or unusual muscle tone
  • Difficulty controlling the head, trunk or posture
  • Delayed motor milestones
  • Movements that look effortful or uncoordinated

Families ask

Good questions.

Who is NDT for?

Mainly children with neurological conditions that affect movement, such as cerebral palsy.

How is NDT different from physiotherapy?

NDT is an approach used within physiotherapy and occupational therapy, with a strong focus on handling and movement quality.

Can parents learn the techniques?

Yes. We teach you positioning and handling to use every day.
