About this therapy

What it is.

Remedial education supports children who are falling behind at school, including those with learning difficulties such as dyslexia, dysgraphia or dyscalculia. Instead of repeating schoolwork, it goes back to the skills a child is missing and builds them up properly.

Lessons move at the child's pace and use multi-sensory methods (seeing, saying, tracing, building) that help learning stick.

Signs it might help

  • Struggling to read or spell despite real effort
  • Reversing letters or numbers beyond the early years
  • Difficulty with basic maths facts
  • Avoiding homework, or calling themselves "stupid"
  • Teachers saying your child is behind

In a session

  2. 02

  4. 04

    Regular, honest updates on how things are going.

Families ask

Good questions.

Is this the same as tuition?

No. Tuition usually covers the current syllabus. Remedial education fills in the underlying skills that make the syllabus hard.

Does my child need a diagnosis?

No. Any child who is struggling can benefit.

How long will my child need it?

It depends on the gaps. We review progress regularly and tell you honestly how things are going.
